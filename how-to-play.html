How to play

Controls, rules and a few tips for each game. Every game also works on touchscreens with on-screen buttons.

Crumb Bound

Turn-based artillery: each player drives a pastry tank and takes turns firing across a scrolling map. Knock out every tank on the other team to win. Play against bots, or make a room for 1v1 and 2v2 matches online.

Controls

← → move and turn · ↑ ↓ aim · hold Space to charge a shot and release to fire · 1 2 3 switch weapons (Shell, Twin and the special shot).

Tips

  • Watch the wind meter: it bends every shot and changes from turn to turn.
  • Lighter shots let you act sooner in the turn order, so a quick Shell can beat a slow special.
  • Slopes tilt your cannon, so move to flat ground when you need a precise angle.
  • Each vehicle has different health, power and aim range. Try a few to find your style.

Bubble Brawl

Drop water balloons in a walled arena. A splash traps a player in a bubble for four seconds, and walking into a trapped rival pops them. If you're splashed again while trapped, you pop. If nobody reaches you, you break free. The last blob standing wins.

Controls

Arrow keys or W A S D to move · Space to drop a balloon · Esc to pause.

Tips

  • Your own balloons trap you too, so always leave yourself a way out.
  • Break crates for powerups: 💧 an extra balloon, 🎯 more blast range, 👟 more speed, 🛡 a shield that blocks one splash.
  • Chain balloons along a corridor to cut off a rival's escape.
  • With 1:30 left the walls start closing in, so head for the middle.

Hamster Roll

Roll your hamster ball through four tracks against the clock for gold, silver and bronze medals, and post your best times to the world leaderboard. Make a room to race friends for placing points on every track.

Controls

← → roll · ↑ or Space jump (press again in the air to double and triple jump) · Shift or X boost · jump against a wall to wall-jump.

Tips

  • ⚡ boosts carry over between tracks, so save them for long straights or big gaps.
  • Firing a boost mid-jump launches you sky-high, which can skip whole sections.
  • Coins you collect go to your wallet even when you don't finish, and buy new looks in the shop.

Frosted Duel

A fast one-on-one 3D arena duel, seen from just behind your fighter. You play Swirl, a pink cupcake with a sprinkle blaster and a butter-knife sword, against Gloomberry, a grumpy blueberry cupcake controlled by the computer. Each round both fighters start with full health. Knock your rival out, or have more health when the 60-second timer runs out, to win the round. The first to win two rounds wins the duel. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard before you start.

Controls

Click the arena to aim with the mouse (press Esc to let go of the mouse and pause). W A S D move · mouse or arrow keys look around · click or J shoot · E or K slash · hold right-click or Q to block · Space jump · Shift dash · R reload. On touchscreens, use the stick on the left to move, drag on the right side to look, and tap the buttons to act. When you aim with the arrow keys or a touchscreen, your blaster helps a little when your rival is near the crosshair.

Moves

  • Blaster: fires sprinkles in bursts of eight, then reloads by itself. Walls and platforms stop sprinkles, so use them as cover.
  • Slash: a close-range sword swing that hits harder than sprinkles and knocks your rival back. Slashing just as sprinkles reach you bats them back at the shooter.
  • Block: holding block raises a shield that stops slashes and most sprinkle damage from the front, but it drains your guard bar (the thin blue bar). If the guard bar empties, you're stunned for a moment. Blocking a normal slash parries it and leaves the attacker dizzy.
  • Dash and lunge: dash for a quick burst of speed, once more in the air. Slash right after a dash to lunge: a longer, stronger slash that breaks through a block.
  • Wall-run and wall-jump: jump at a wall and keep moving. Moving straight into it runs up it, and moving along it runs sideways across it. Press jump while on a wall to kick off the other way.

Tips

  • Grab the floating cupcakes that appear around the arena. Each one restores 25 health.
  • If your rival keeps blocking, dash in and lunge. If they keep lunging, block and parry.
  • Wall-run up the middle tower or jump up the crates to the side platforms, then shoot down from above.

Ryan's Cake TD

Tower defense: the Gloom marches along the path toward your cake. Plant guardians beside the path to stop it, survive ten waves to win, then keep going in endless mode for a higher score. There are three maps and several difficulty levels.

Controls

Choose a guardian, then click green ground to plant it, and right-click or Esc to put it away. On a touchscreen, tap to aim, then confirm. Water is off limits except for the floating lily pads, which hold one guardian each. Bramble Spikes go directly on the path.

1–9 choose a guardian · 0 Candlecap · Space sends the next wave · P pauses. With the field focused, use the arrow keys and Enter to plant, or L and Enter for a lily pad.

Tips

  • Every wave is tougher than the last, so spend gold on upgrades as well as new guardians.
  • Some enemies are camouflaged. A Candlecap guardian reveals them to the guardians nearby.
  • A guardian's range shows while you place it and when you select it, so use it to cover bends in the path.
  • On Sugar Serpent the Gloom hops through a pair of candy portals, skipping the stretch between them. Spikes can't go on a portal, and the island inside the roundabout reaches the path on every side.
