Crumb Bound guide

Crumb Bound is a turn-based artillery game where every tank is a pastry and the battlefield is a giant layer cake. You move, aim, charge a shot and let the wind carry it into the other team. It suits anyone who likes a calm, thinking game, from kids learning angles to grown-ups who remember classic artillery games.

Play Crumb Bound now, free in your browser

How a match works

  1. Type your name, then pick a vehicle on the menu. You can also pick Random and let the game choose.
  2. Press Play vs Bot and pick an opponent, or make or join an online room.
  3. Each team starts on its own flat plateau. The battlefield is wider than the screen and has two or three floating cake islands in the sky.
  4. Teams take turns, always alternating. You get 20 seconds to move a short way, aim and fire. If the clock runs out, your shot is lost and the turn passes.
  5. Shots carve craters in the cake and push nearby vehicles a little. A vehicle that falls off the map or through the bottom of a deep crater is knocked out. There is no fall damage.
  6. After 16 turns, Sudden Death starts: all damage goes up by half and blasts get a little bigger.
  7. When one team has no vehicles left, the other team wins and you earn gold for the shop.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D: drive and turn around. Driving uses up your move distance for the turn, but turning is free.
  • ↑ ↓ or W S: raise or lower your cannon.
  • Hold Space to charge, release to fire. The power bar fills to the top and then sinks again, so let go at the right moment.
  • 1 2 3: pick your first weapon, your Twin weapon or your special.
  • Click an item button above the weapons to arm it before you fire.
  • Drag the battlefield with the mouse to look around before you shoot.

Touchscreens

Turn your phone sideways. Use the ◀ and ▶ buttons to drive, ▲ and ▼ to aim, and hold the big FIRE button to charge. Tap the weapon and item buttons to choose them, and drag the battlefield with a finger to look around.

Modes

Play vs Bot

A one-on-one match against a computer player. There are five, shown here in menu order:

  • Baby Batter drives a Shortcake with shaky aim and 30% less damage. A good first opponent.
  • Bot Baker drives a Shortcake and plays a balanced game with no big strengths or weaknesses.
  • Professor Proof rides a Meringue with 30% more health but moves less. It likes high ground and saves its special for a finishing blow.
  • Loose Cannon rides a Fudge with 25% bigger blasts and wild aim, and it fires its second weapon often.
  • Deadeye Dot rides an Éclair and barely misses, but deals 20% less damage.

Online rooms

Press Create Room to get a four-letter code, then share the code or the invite link. Everyone picks a vehicle and Team A or Team B, then taps Ready. The host can start once everyone is ready and the teams are even, either 1v1 or 2v2. If the host leaves mid-match, another player takes over as host so the match can go on.

Vehicles, weapons and weather

Vehicles

  • Shortcake (100 health): the all-rounder. Shell, Twin, and Berry Barrage, a spread of five shells.
  • Éclair (90 health): the sniper. Its Bolts pierce through vehicles, and its special, Long Lance, is a strong beam.
  • Meringue (80 health): the fastest mover with a wide firing angle. Puff, a curving Boomerang, and Meringue Storm, which bursts into six bomblets.
  • Fudge (130 health): the slow, tough bruiser with a narrow firing angle. Heavy Bomb, Mega Bomb and the huge Fudgequake.
  • Random: rolls one of the four. About one roll in twenty gives the secret Dragon Fruit Torte, with 150 health and fiery shots.

Weapon rules

  • Your first weapon is always ready.
  • Your second weapon (Twin on the Shortcake) rests for your next turn after you use it.
  • Your special charges when you deal or take damage, plus about a third each turn you fire, so it is usually full in about three turns.

Weather

Weather changes every few turns, and the top bar shows what comes next.

  • Calm: nothing extra.
  • Thor: a satellite zaps any shot that lands near it, hurting whoever is close. It gets stronger the more damage it does.
  • Force: a glowing beam stands on the field. Shots that pass through it hit a third harder.
  • Dark: all shots do half damage.
  • Eclipse: specials charge twice as fast.
  • Moon: each vehicle heals 4 health at the start of its turn.

Items and gear

You start with 500 gold and a free Teleport and Band-Aid. The shop sells battle items such as Dual (fire twice), Power Up, Thunderbolt, Medipak and Wind Change. You can equip up to six slots of items. Gear such as Sword, Shield and Heart gives a small lasting boost, up to four at once. Items can't be used with your special.

Tips and strategy

  • Hit the body. A direct hit does 125% damage. A blast that only clips a vehicle at the edge can do as little as 30%.
  • Trust the wind meter. Wind changes by no more than 4 mph a turn, so the fix that worked last turn is still close.
  • Stay in your aim range. Each vehicle has a comfortable firing angle. Shots fired outside it lose 20% of their damage.
  • Mind the slope. Your vehicle leans with the ground and so does the cannon. Drive to flat ground when you need a precise angle.
  • Plan around the rest. Use your second weapon when the rival is in reach, then expect to use your first weapon next turn.
  • Save the special for good weather. Fire it in Force or Calm, not in Dark, where it would do half damage.
  • Watch the Force beam. Lob your shot through it for a third more damage.
  • Keep away from Thor. Its laser hurts anyone near the landing spot, including you.
  • Dig, don't just hit. A crater under a rival can trap them, and a deep enough hole drops them off the map.
  • Look before you shoot. Drag the map to find a far rival and check islands in the way.
  • Don't run the clock down. At zero seconds your turn is gone.

Frequently asked questions

Is Crumb Bound free?

Yes. It plays in your web browser with nothing to download or buy. Gold is earned only by playing.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. On-screen buttons appear on touchscreens. Turn the phone sideways so the whole battlefield fits.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a room, send the four-letter code or invite link, and play 1v1 or 2v2 online. Room chat only reaches other players when you are signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. Your gold, items and wins are saved in your browser. Signing in is optional and keeps your progress on any device.

How does the leaderboard work?

Wins against bots count toward the world board for most wins. Type your own name first, because the fun default names never post. Online wins don't count.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. The tanks are cakes and pastries, knocked-out vehicles simply crumble, and there is nothing scary or gory.
