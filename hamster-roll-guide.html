Hamster Roll guide

Hamster Roll is a side-scrolling racing game where you steer a hamster in a rolling ball across four bright tracks. Each track has gold, silver and bronze times to beat, and you can race up to seven friends online. It is easy to pick up for younger players, and there is always a faster line for anyone who wants to chase the clock.

How a match works

  1. Type your name, then press Practice Solo, or make or join a room.
  2. A match is all four tracks in a row: Meadow Dash, Pogo Cliffs, Gusher Gulch and Sky Steps.
  3. Each track starts with a 3, 2, 1, GO countdown. The clock counts up, and the medal box shows how long you have left for the next medal.
  4. Every track has a time limit. With 30 seconds left a HURRY banner appears. If time runs out, the track counts as not finished (DNF).
  5. Fall off the track and you go back to your last checkpoint, a little dizzy for a moment.
  6. Cross the finish line to see your time, your medal and the world's best times for that track.
  7. After the last track, the match results add up your total time and any coins you earned.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D: roll left and right. Pushing against your roll brakes.
  • ↑, W or Space: jump. Press again in the air to double jump, and once more to triple jump.
  • Jump while touching a wall to wall-jump off it.
  • Shift or X: fire a stored ⚡ boost.
  • Esc: pause a solo race, or leave an online race.
  • Enter: open race chat in an online room.

Touchscreens

Turn your phone sideways. Hold ◀ or ▶ to roll, and slide your thumb between them to change direction. Tap ▲ to jump and ⚡ to boost.

Modes

Practice Solo

Race all four tracks on your own, one after another. After each track you can go on to the next or return to the menu. The game saves your best time and best medal for every track, plus your best total for the whole match. Your medals show on the menu.

Online rooms

Press Create Room for a four-letter code, then share the code or the invite link. Up to eight racers can join, and every hamster in a room gets its own color. The host starts the match. Everyone races the same track at once, and you can bump other balls out of the way.

Each track awards placing points: 10 for first, then 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1. A racer who doesn't finish gets none. When everyone is done, the next track starts a few seconds later. Whoever has the most points after four tracks wins, and total time breaks a tie.

The tracks

1. Meadow Dash

Rolling green hills, bouncy bumpers, boost pads and one big loop. Time limit 2:30. Medals: gold 29s, silver 37s, bronze 50s. It has the most ⚡ orbs of any track, so fill up here.

2. Pogo Cliffs

Cliffs under a sunset sky with lots of small ledges to hop between, a curved guide rail, a giant double loop and a tube cannon that fires you onward. Time limit 2:30. Medals: gold 27s, silver 34s, bronze 46s.

3. Gusher Gulch

A pink canyon with water geysers that switch on and off. Roll into a live geyser and it carries you up, then jump out at the top. The finish line sits high up. Time limit 1:40, the shortest of the four. Medals: gold 28s, silver 37s, bronze 50s.

4. Sky Steps

Platforms floating above a sea of clouds, with bumpers, a double loop, a tube cannon and crumbling platforms. Time limit 2:30. Medals: gold 16s, silver 21s, bronze 30s. It's the shortest track, so every mistake shows.

Track features

  • ⚡ Boost orbs: you can store up to five. They carry over from track to track within a match.
  • Coins: each one adds to your wallet, even if you don't finish.
  • Hills: you roll faster downhill than on flat ground.
  • Loops: roll in fast along the ground to ride all the way round. You can jump out partway.
  • Tube cannons: roll into the mouth and the tube shoots you out at speed. You can't jump inside.
  • Bumpers: round pinball bumpers that bounce you away hard.
  • Boost pads: arrows on the floor that kick you forward.
  • Crumbling platforms: stand on one too long and it shakes, then drops away. It comes back a few seconds later.

Tips and strategy

  • Keep your speed. The ball coasts with little friction, so let go of the keys on downhills instead of braking.
  • Boost in the air for height. A boost mid-jump launches you high and a little faster forward, which can skip whole sections.
  • Boost on the ground for distance. On flat ground a boost is a fast dash in the direction you face.
  • Save boosts for later tracks. They carry over, and Gusher Gulch has the fewest orbs and the tightest time limit.
  • Hit loops fast. If you arrive too slowly you won't be pulled in. A boost inside a loop speeds up the ride.
  • Mind the walls. Hitting a wall at speed bounces you back. A wall-jump turns that wall into a step instead.
  • Slide down walls to aim. Holding toward a wall in the air slows your fall, giving you time to line up a wall-jump.
  • Time the geysers. Each one sprays for a second and a half, then rests for a second and a half. Wait at the edge for the next burst.
  • Keep moving on crumbling platforms. They start shaking after less than a second, so hop off quickly.
  • Watch the medal box. It counts down to the next medal, so you always know how close you are.
  • Grab coins even on a bad run. They go to your wallet whether or not you finish, and they buy new looks.

The shop

Coins buy looks only, so they never make your hamster faster. Balls: Bubble (free), Glass, Candy Stripe, Frosted Cake, Galaxy and Gold. Fur colors: Cherry and Minty (free), then Sunny, Grape, Lime, Bubblegum, Sky, Calico and Snow. In a room, if a player who joined before you has the same fur color, you get the next free color.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hamster Roll free?

Yes. It runs in your web browser with nothing to download or buy. Coins are earned only by racing.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Touch buttons appear on touchscreens. Turn the phone sideways to see more of the track.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a room, share the code or invite link, and up to eight people can race together. Chat only reaches other players when you are signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. Your coins, shop items and best times are saved in your browser. Signing in is optional and keeps them on any device.

How does the leaderboard work?

Each track has its own world board of the fastest times. When you finish a track, your time posts if you typed your own name on the menu. The fun default names never post.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. It's a cheerful hamster racing through meadows and clouds, with nothing scary. Falling off just sends you back to a checkpoint.
