Swordplay Isle

A point-and-click pirate adventure in four scenes
Pippa Wren has dreamed of the pirate life since she was knee-high to a barnacle. Now she's finally reached Swordplay Isle — and the legendary Captain Marisol Vane has agreed to consider her for a crew spot... if Pippa can pass three trials: out-wit the duelist Barnacle Bess in an insult swordfight, reassemble the captain's torn treasure map, and brew her legendary sea stew.

Click around each scene to Look, Take, and Talk. Combine items in your inventory, mind what the locals tell you — and when in doubt, ask Waffles the parrot for a nudge.
🖱️ Mouse / touch: click anything interesting. A menu offers Look, Take, or Talk. Click an inventory item to select it, then click a hotspot (or another item) to use / combine it.
⌨️ Keyboard: Tab / ←→ move between hotspots & inventory, Enter activates, Esc closes menus.
💾 Your voyage auto-saves as you play. The 🦜 button gives hints. This is a classic point-and-click adventure: look, take, talk, and combine!
An original tale. No parrots were harmed in the making of this adventure.
