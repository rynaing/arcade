What's new

Every change to the arcade, newest first. Games update often, so check back for new levels, towers and modes.

October 9, 2026

Crumb Bound: lobby fits on phones

The online lobby in Crumb Bound now fills a phone screen held upright, so every player slot, vehicle and the ready button are in view and scroll smoothly.

Ryan's Cake TD: Cake Blimps fly

In Ryan's Cake TD, Cake Blimps and their mini-blimps now float over the path, so Burr Nest burrs and Bramble Spikes can't hit them. Bring other guardians for the blimp waves.

Frosted Duel: aim by turning your phone

On phones, tap Tilt aim in Frosted Duel and turn your phone to look around and aim, like a window into the arena. Dragging to look still works alongside it, and the game remembers your choice.

Ryan's Cake TD: a twistier Sugar Serpent

The Sugar Serpent map in Ryan's Cake TD has a brand-new path: tight switchbacks, a pair of swirly candy portals the Gloom hops through, and a roundabout where the path loops around an island and crosses itself on the way out.

October 9, 2026

My page, saved bests, and chat for signed-in players

Your best times, top tower defense scores and wins now show on My page. Bests you set as a guest are kept when you sign in. Chat in online rooms is now for signed-in players. In Ryan's Cake TD, the exit button is now just Menu.

October 9, 2026

Optional accounts: one name and your progress everywhere

Tap Sign in at the top of any game to make a free account with your email. Your player name, Crumb Bound gold and gear, Hamster Roll coins and best times, Frosted Duel record and settings then follow you to any device, and the name you pick shows in every game. You can still play without an account.

October 8, 2026

Gameplay clips on How to play

Every game on the How to play page now has a short looping clip: a Heavy Bomb landing in Crumb Bound, a bubble trap and pop in Bubble Brawl, a boost through the loop in Hamster Roll, a spinning lunge in Frosted Duel, and towers holding off the Gloom in Ryan's Cake TD.

October 7, 2026

Smoother on phones

Crumb Bound now runs much faster on phones, a day after the same speed-up for Bubble Brawl. The cake-layer terrain, sky and arena are drawn once and reused instead of being redrawn every frame, so matches stay smooth on older devices.

October 6, 2026

Ryan's Cake TD: tougher limitless mode and new tower looks

Limitless mode in Ryan's Cake TD keeps getting harder after wave 30, so high scores mean more. The Burr Nest now lays spikes on every path branch in range and has a cuter, smiling look. The Gilded Gateau gets new decorations at every upgrade level, and tower cards show damage, attack speed and range.

Fun default names and steadier online rooms

Players who haven't typed a name get a friendly one like "Sunny Otter". Online rooms in Crumb Bound, Bubble Brawl and Hamster Roll now share one connection system, which keeps the host and guests in the same match. In Bubble Brawl, when only bots are left, everyone in the room can choose to end the round.

October 5, 2026

New game: Frosted Duel

Frosted Duel is a fast 3D one-on-one arena duel. Play as Swirl, a pink cupcake with a sprinkle blaster and a butter-knife sword, against the grumpy Gloomberry. Dash, wall-run, block and lunge your way to two round wins. See how to play.

Berry Crossing gets a fork in the road

The Berry Crossing map in Ryan's Cake TD now splits into two lanes near the cake, and each enemy picks a branch at random, so you have to defend both.

October 4, 2026

Hamster Roll: Gusher Gulch rebuilt

Gusher Gulch in Hamster Roll is now built around timed geysers. Every hamster in an online race gets a different colour, and all four tracks have their own theme, medals and tougher sections.

Crumb Bound: fairer turns and a welcome gift

Teams now always alternate turns. Every player gets one free Teleport and one Band-Aid, vehicles stuck in craters can climb out, and the lobby shows each player's ready state and full vehicle stats.

Bubble Brawl: sudden death and new powerups

With 1:30 left the arena walls close in. New powerups, smarter bots and fresh art make every round feel different.

October 2, 2026

World leaderboards and the studio look

Every game got a shared look, a cast of characters and one mute button. World leaderboards arrived for Ryan's Cake TD, Hamster Roll best times, and most wins against bots in Crumb Bound and Bubble Brawl. Hamster Roll added a coin shop with ball skins and hamster furs.
