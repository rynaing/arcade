Bubble Brawl guide

Bubble Brawl is a fast arena game for four blobs. You drop water balloons, trap rivals in bubbles, then bump into them to pop them. Rounds take three minutes at most, so it works well for a quick game alone against bots or a silly match with family and friends.

How a match works

  1. Four blobs start in the four corners of a walled arena. Solid pillars stand in a grid, and wooden crates fill most of the open space.
  2. Everyone starts with one balloon at a time and a splash that reaches one square.
  3. A balloon pops three seconds after you drop it. The splash shoots out in a plus shape. Pillars stop it, and it breaks the first crate it reaches.
  4. A blob caught in a splash is trapped in a bubble for four seconds. A free blob that walks into it pops it. A second splash pops it too.
  5. If nobody reaches a trapped blob in time, the bubble bursts, the blob shouts "Free!" and gets a moment of safety.
  6. With 1:30 left, Sudden Death begins. New walls drop in from the edges, one square at a time, spiraling toward the middle.
  7. The last blob standing wins. If the clock runs out with more than one blob left, nobody wins the round.

Controls

Keyboard

  • Arrow keys or W A S D: move.
  • Space: drop a water balloon where you stand.
  • Esc or P: pause and resume, in solo games.
  • Enter: play again from the end screen.

Touchscreens

Use the arrow pad on the left to move. You can slide your thumb from one arrow to another without lifting it. Tap the 💧 button to drop a balloon.

Modes

Solo vs bots

You play against three bots: Bloop, Splash and Drizzle. Pick how tough they are before you start, and the game remembers your choice.

  • Easy: bots move slower than you, stop to think, use only two balloons at a time and don't chase you.
  • Normal: bots move at your speed, hunt you down and rush over to pop trapped blobs.
  • Hard: bots move a little faster than you, spot trapped blobs from further away and aim at you more often.

In solo play you score 250 points for each bot you pop. Winning adds 1,000 points plus 2 points for every second left on the clock. Switching to another tab pauses a solo game for you.

Online rooms

Press Create Room for a four-letter code, then share the code or the invite link. Up to four people can join, and bots fill any empty seats. Everyone gets their own color and hat, so you can always tell blobs apart. The host starts the match. Online matches can't be paused.

If every real player is popped and two or more bots are still going, everyone is asked whether to end the round. Any one player can end it, and the bot with the most pops wins. You can also keep watching.

Power-ups

Each crate you break has a chance to leave a power-up behind. Walk over it to pick it up. A popped blob also scatters four power-ups around the spot where it popped. The bar at the bottom of the screen shows your balloons, range and speed.

  • 💧 Extra balloon: one more balloon on the field at a time, up to eight.
  • 🎯 Blast range: your splash reaches one square further in each direction, up to eight.
  • 👟 Speed: you move faster. It stacks up to five times.
  • 🛡 Shield: blocks the next splash that would trap you, then breaks.

When the crates run out

If every crate is gone and the round drags on for another minute, wild balloons start appearing around the arena every two seconds, and a fresh power-up appears with each one. Standing still is never safe for long.

Tips and strategy

  • Plan your exit first. Your own balloons trap you too, and bots rush over to pop a trapped blob. Before you drop, know which corner you will duck around.
  • Balloons are walls. Nobody can walk through a balloon, including you. One balloon at a corridor's end can block a rival's only way out.
  • Use chain reactions. A splash that reaches another balloon pops it at once. A line of balloons can go off in a flash, much faster than three seconds.
  • Rush to trapped rivals. A trapped blob is stuck for four seconds. Walk into it before a bot does, or it breaks free.
  • Watch the bubble timer. If you are the one trapped, stay calm. You come out with a short moment of safety, so use it to run.
  • Get range early. 🎯 lets you hit rivals from behind crates and down long lanes.
  • A shield is a second chance. With 🛡 on, you can take a bigger risk, but it only blocks one splash.
  • Collect the leftovers. When a blob pops, grab its four power-ups quickly, but look for ticking balloons first.
  • Head inward at 1:30. The next squares to close are marked. A wall that lands on you pops you.
  • Don't fuss over lining up. Movement slides you around pillar corners, so you can turn into a side lane without stopping in the exact middle of a square.
  • Start on Easy. Easy bots don't chase you, which makes it a good place to learn balloon timing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bubble Brawl free?

Yes. It runs in your web browser with nothing to download or buy.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. A touch pad and a balloon button appear on touchscreens, and the arena resizes to fit your screen.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Make a room and share the code or invite link. Up to four players can join, and bots fill the rest. Room chat only reaches other players when you are signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. Anyone can play solo or join a room. Signing in is optional.

How does the leaderboard work?

Solo wins against bots count toward the world board for most wins. Type your own name on the menu first, because the fun default names never post. Online wins don't count.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. It's water balloons and bubbles. Popped blobs burst into confetti, and there is nothing scary.
