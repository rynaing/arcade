    How long can the lantern hold?

    Every wave brings more and tougher Gloom. Build, upgrade, and survive for as long as you can.

    Choose a guardian, then plant on green ground. Water is off limits except for the floating lily pads, which each hold one guardian. Bramble Spikes go directly on the path. Browser shortcuts: 1–9 choose, Space sends a wave, P pauses; focus the field and use arrows + Enter for open ground, or L + Enter for a lily pad.

    Tip: guardian range appears while placing or when selected. Every wave gets tougher, so upgrade before the Gloom accelerates.
