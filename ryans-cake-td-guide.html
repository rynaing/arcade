Ryan's Cake TD guide

Ryan's Cake TD is a tower defense game. A creeping crowd called the Gloom marches along a path toward your cake, and you plant guardians beside the path to stop it. It is calm enough for younger players on Chill and tough enough on Nightmare to keep strategy fans busy.

Play Ryan's Cake TD now, free in your browser

How a run works

  1. Type a challenger name, pick a difficulty and a map, and press Start run.
  2. Spend your starting gold on guardians, then send wave 1 when you are ready.
  3. Each defeated Gloom drops gold and points. Clearing a wave adds a bonus of 250 points times the wave number.
  4. Any Gloom that reaches the end of the path takes a bite of your cake. Bosses take much bigger bites. If the cake runs out, the run ends.
  5. Survive 10 waves to save the cake and earn up to three stars, based on how much cake is left. Then bank your score or keep going in endless mode.

Controls

Choose a guardian from the tray, then click open grass to plant it and confirm. Guardians can't go too close to the path, in water, or on top of each other. The floating lily pads hold one guardian each. Bramble Spikes are the opposite: they go right on the path. Right-click or Esc puts the guardian away.

Keys: 1 Capshot, 2 Acorn Cannon, 3 Moonray, 4 Thornwhirl, 5 Dewdrop, 6 Bramble Spikes, 7 Burr Nest, 8 Farcap, 9 Ironbark Titan and 0 Candlecap. Space sends the next wave and P pauses. You can also plant with the arrow keys and Enter, or press L to jump to a lily pad.

On a touchscreen, tap where you want a guardian, then confirm. Buttons along the board change game speed (×1 to ×4), pause, and turn auto-send on or off. With auto-send on, the next wave starts five seconds after the last one is cleared.

Tap or click a planted guardian to upgrade it or remove it. Removing gives back 60% of everything you spent on it.

Modes

Difficulty

  • Chill: the most starting gold, extra gold per kill, softer Gloom and 10 cake.
  • Classic: the standard game with 10 cake.
  • Nightmare: only 5 cake, tougher Gloom and waves that arrive faster.

Maps

  • Meadow Loop: one long winding path with lots of bends, a good map to learn on.
  • Sugar Serpent: the path snakes up and down, and a pair of candy portals lets the Gloom skip a stretch. Spikes can't go on a portal.
  • Berry Crossing: the path splits at the top, so each Gloom picks one of two routes before they join again.

Endless and limitless

After wave 10 you can keep going for as long as the cake lasts, and the wave counter switches to ∞. Every wave keeps getting tougher. After wave 30 the Gloom's health grows even faster each wave, but gold per kill doesn't speed up with it, so late waves need strong upgrades.

Guardians and the Gloom

Every guardian can be upgraded up to level 8, and each upgrade costs more than the last. When you upgrade from level 2 to 3, every guardian except Candlecap asks you to choose one of two specializations, and the other locks for good. Some guardians unlock at later waves, shown below with their price.

Guardians

  • Capshot (45 gold): fast single-target berries. Paths: Orchard Guard (heavy, longer reach) or Berry Storm (rapid fire).
  • Acorn Cannon (80): splash blasts with a brief stun. Paths: Siege Mortar (huge splash, slow) or Flak Battery (rapid, longer stun).
  • Moonray (105, wave 5): a beam that hits every Gloom in a line. Paths: Lunar Lance (huge beam) or Prism Array (twin split beams).
  • Thornwhirl (55, wave 3): very fast, short range. At level 5 it hurls the furthest Gloom in range back to the start every 20 seconds. Paths: Worldbriar or Petal Cyclone.
  • Dewdrop (70): slows the Gloom it hits. Paths: Glacier (deep freeze) or Monsoon (rapid, wide reach).
  • Bramble Spikes (35): a trap on the path with a set amount of damage that vanishes when spent. Paths: Ironroot or Scatterfield.
  • Burr Nest (90, wave 3): keeps laying burr patches on nearby path. Paths: Swarm Host (more burrs) or Sticky Burrs (burrs slow).
  • Farcap (300, wave 12): a slow, heavy shot that reaches the whole map. Paths: Deadeye or Pepperbox.
  • Ironbark Titan (5,000, wave 20): rapid bolts with wide splash. Paths: Juggernaut or Stormcaller.
  • Gilded Gateau (30,000, wave 30): the golden masterpiece, huge damage and splash. Paths: Midas Touch or Grand Patissier.
  • Candlecap (70, wave 5): no attack, but it lights up Camo Gloom nearby so other guardians can hit them.

The Gloom

  • Everyday Gloom: plain ones, small speedy ones and big slow ones make up most of every wave.
  • Cake Pop (from wave 3): every few seconds it pops a stun cloud that switches off guardians close to it for a moment.
  • Veilcap (from wave 5): only Moonray, Dewdrop and Farcap can see it.
  • Big Gloom: a boss every fifth wave, with lots of health.
  • Armored Gloom (from wave 8): Acorn Cannon, Ironbark Titan and Gilded Gateau only do a quarter of their damage to it.
  • Camo Gloom (from wave 12): invisible until a Candlecap lights it up.
  • Mirror Glaze (from wave 14): its shiny shell cuts Moonray damage to a quarter.
  • Elder Gloom: the biggest boss, every tenth wave. Letting one through costs a lot of cake.
  • Cake Blimp: also every tenth wave. It flies over spikes and burrs, and pops into faster mini-blimps when beaten.

Tips and strategy

  • Upgrade early. Every wave has more Gloom with more health, so one strong guardian often beats two weak ones.
  • Cover bends. A guardian on the inside of a corner reaches the path for longer.
  • Mix your damage. Capshot and Thornwhirl handle Armored Gloom, while Capshot and Acorn Cannon handle Mirror Glaze.
  • Have a Moonray, Dewdrop or Farcap in place by wave 5, or Veilcaps will walk straight past everything else.
  • Plant a Candlecap before wave 12, where it can light up a long stretch of path.
  • Spread your guardians out. A Cake Pop only stuns guardians close to it, and Farcap can shoot it from anywhere.
  • Get ready for every tenth wave. Spikes and burrs can't touch Cake Blimps, and Acorn Cannon splash is great against the mini-blimps.
  • Bramble Spikes are cheap but run out. Put them where the Gloom bunches up, and replace them when they go.
  • A Dewdrop before a group of damage guardians keeps the Gloom in range longer.
  • Turn auto-send off while you plan, and use ×2 to ×4 speed on easy waves.
  • Save up for Farcap at wave 12 and Ironbark Titan at wave 20. They carry endless runs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ryan's Cake TD free?

Yes. It runs in your web browser, with nothing to download.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. You tap to place and confirm guardians. The Landscape button turns the board sideways for a wider view.

How does the leaderboard work?

Each difficulty and map has its own world board. Type your own name before you start and your score posts when the run ends. The "This couch" tab keeps scores from this device, for taking turns at home.

Do I need an account?

No. Just type a name. A free arcade account is optional and keeps your name the same across devices.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. The Gloom are cartoon blobs, there is no chat, and the only thing other people see is your name and score.
