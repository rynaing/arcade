About the arcade

Ryan Cake Studios is a one-person game studio run by Ryan Naing. Every game on cakecade.com is designed, drawn and coded here, then published free to play in the browser. There are no downloads, accounts or sign-ups: open a game and you're playing.

The games are built for all ages and for playing together. Most work both solo against computer opponents and online with friends, using a short room code that anyone can join from a phone, tablet or computer.

The games

  • Crumb Bound: turn-based artillery with pastry tanks. Aim, read the wind, and knock out the other team, solo or 1v1 and 2v2 online.
  • Bubble Brawl: an arena battler where water balloons trap rivals in bubbles. Last blob standing wins.
  • Hamster Roll: 2D hamster-ball racing across four tracks, for medals and world times or against friends.
  • Ryan's Cake TD: tower defense. Plant guardians, survive ten waves, then climb the endless leaderboard.
  • Game Night: party games for a big screen, like trivia, linked from the home page.

Free to play

The arcade is supported by ads, which may appear between rounds or on the home page. They're never placed on top of game controls. See the privacy page for what that means for your data.
