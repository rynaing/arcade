Frosted Duel guide

Frosted Duel is a quick one-on-one 3D arena fight between two cupcakes. You play Swirl, a pink cupcake with a sprinkle blaster and a butter-knife sword, and you face Gloomberry, a grumpy blueberry cupcake run by the computer. It suits anyone who likes short, fast matches, and a full duel takes just a few minutes.

How a match works

  1. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the menu and press Duel! The game remembers your last choice.
  2. Each round starts with a short countdown. Both cupcakes begin at opposite ends of the arena with full health, a full guard bar and eight sprinkles loaded.
  3. A round lasts 60 seconds. Knock your rival's health to zero to take the round. If the clock runs out, whoever has more health wins it. Equal health is a draw, and nobody scores.
  4. The first cupcake to win two rounds wins the duel. If draws keep happening, the duel stops after five rounds and the round score decides it.
  5. At the end you see your accuracy, slashes landed, parries, deflects and guard breaks, plus your total wins, current streak and best streak.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

Click the arena first so the game can use your mouse to aim. W A S D move. Move the mouse to look, or use the arrow keys to turn and look up and down. Click or press J to shoot. E or K slashes. Hold right-click, Q or L to block. Space jumps, Shift dashes and R reloads early. Esc or P pauses, and Esc also lets go of the mouse.

Touchscreens

A stick on the left moves you. Drag anywhere on the right side to look. Round buttons on the right shoot, slash, block, dash, jump and pause. On phones with a motion sensor, tap Tilt aim to aim by turning the phone. Dragging still works with it on.

If you aim with the arrow keys or a touchscreen, your sprinkles get a small nudge toward your rival when they are close to the crosshair and nothing is in the way.

Modes

There is one mode, a best-of-three duel against the computer, with three difficulty levels. On Easy, Gloomberry reacts slowly, misses more, rarely blocks and never bats your sprinkles back. Normal is a fair fight. On Hard, Gloomberry reacts quickly, aims well, fires faster, blocks most of your slashes, dodges more, and sometimes slashes your sprinkles straight back at you.

Moves and the arena

Sprinkle blaster

Each sprinkle does a little damage, and you get eight before an automatic reload of just over a second. You can't fire while blocking, slashing or reloading, so watch the sprinkle counter under your health bar.

Slash

A close sword swing that does much more damage than a sprinkle and knocks your rival back with a short stun. Time a slash just as sprinkles reach you and you deflect them back at the shooter, and they hit a bit harder on the way back.

Dash and lunge

A dash is a short burst of speed in the direction you are moving. You get one more dash in the air before you land. Slash right after a dash to lunge: a longer, stronger swing with extra reach that breaks through a block.

Block, parry and guard

Holding block puts up a shield in front of you and slows you down. It stops slashes and sprinkles from the front, but each blocked sprinkle chips your guard bar, and holding block drains it too. If the guard bar empties, you are stunned for a moment. Blocking a normal slash is a parry: the attacker is pushed back and left dizzy. A lunge, though, breaks your guard instead.

Wall-run and wall-jump

Jump at a wall and keep moving into it to run up it, or along it to run sideways. Press jump while on a wall to kick away from it. The wall-run lasts a short while, then refills when you land.

The arena and health cupcakes

The square arena has a layered tower in the middle, four tall pillars, two raised side decks and four low crates you can hop onto. Walls, pillars and platforms block sprinkles. About 12 seconds into each round a floating health cupcake appears, either on top of the middle tower, on one of the side decks or in a corner. Grabbing it restores 25 health. Once someone takes it, the next one appears 15 seconds later.

Tips and strategy

  • Count your sprinkles. Firing your last one starts a reload, so it is often better to press R during a quiet moment than to run dry mid-fight.
  • Don't hold block all the time. Your guard drains while you hold it, and it only refills when you let go.
  • If Gloomberry turtles behind a shield, dash in and slash for a lunge. It breaks the guard and leaves them stunned.
  • If Gloomberry keeps rushing you, block the slash to parry it, then hit back while they are dizzy.
  • Slashing as sprinkles arrive sends them back harder. It takes practice, but it is very satisfying on Hard.
  • Use the pillars and the middle tower as cover. Sprinkles can't pass through them, so break line of sight when your health is low.
  • Get high. Run up the middle tower or hop the crates onto a side deck and shoot down. Gloomberry will try to climb after you, which gives you free shots.
  • When your health drops, look for the cupcake. Gloomberry also heads for it when hurt, so try to get there first or guard it.
  • Keep moving sideways while you shoot. A strafing target is harder to hit than one standing still.
  • With seconds left and more health than your rival, play it safe. The round goes to whoever has more health at zero.
  • Use your air dash to escape after a wall-jump or to cross a gap quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Frosted Duel free?

Yes. It plays free in your web browser, with nothing to download or install.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Touchscreens get a move stick, a look area and action buttons, and phones with a motion sensor can use Tilt aim. If a device runs slowly, the game turns down shadows and detail on its own to keep things smooth.

Can I play against a friend?

Not right now. Frosted Duel is a single-player duel against Gloomberry.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wins and streaks are saved in your browser. A free arcade account is optional and keeps that record when you switch devices.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. It is cartoon cupcakes throwing sprinkles. There is no blood, no chat and no other players.
